Version 6.7.0 rebuilds the settings dashboard so every setting sits with the thing it changes.

- **One card per element:** each on-screen element has its switch at the top and everything for it underneath, in every module.
- Search forgives typos, understands synonyms and remembers what you looked for.
- Sub-settings slide in when you turn their switch on, and a marker shows which settings you have changed from their defaults.
- A new Welcome page, matching module icons, and News stories you can open.
